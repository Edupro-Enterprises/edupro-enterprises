## ZIMSEC Heritage-Based Curriculum

Zimbabwe's Heritage-Based Curriculum (2024 to 2030) changes what and how schools teach. Edupro helps schools run it:

- **Timetabling** for the new learning areas and periods
- **Assessment and reports** that record continuous assessment alongside examinations
- **Study Room** resources arranged by level and learning area, from ECD to Form 6
- **Moodle LMS** for lessons, assignments and revision
- **Training** for school heads, teachers and bursars

We also support the Cambridge IGCSE, AS and A Level curricula.

*Edupro is an independent supplier. Our materials are designed to support schools following the ZIMSEC Heritage-Based Curriculum and are not issued or endorsed by ZIMSEC or the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education.*
