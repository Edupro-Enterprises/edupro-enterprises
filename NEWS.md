## 2026-11-18: Edupro supports the ZIMSEC Heritage-Based Curriculum

**Category:** Product update

**Summary:** The Edupro School Management System is fully compliant with the ZIMSEC Heritage-Based Curriculum and supports the Cambridge IGCSE, AS and A Level pathways.

### What schools can do
- Set up timetables for the new learning areas and periods (TTS-300)
- Record continuous assessment and examination marks, and print report cards (RPT-800)
- Give learners resources by level and learning area on [Edupro Study Room](https://studyroom.edupro.co.zw), from ECD to Form 6
- Deliver lessons and assignments on Moodle LMS (LMS-200)
- Train school heads, teachers and bursars (TRN-1000)
